## Short answer

**AI customer service agent: R$1,000 per month on average.** **Customer service platform with CRM: R$600 per month.** **Custom development — application, dashboard, platform — is quoted per project.**

These are Etter's 2026 prices for small and medium Brazilian companies. Below is what each covers and what moves the number.

## What the monthly fee covers

- The agent trained on your knowledge base
- The operation running, monitored
- Continuous tuning as conversations it got wrong show up
- Conversation dashboard and service reporting

Continuous tuning is the part people underestimate. An agent delivered and abandoned degrades: the business changes prices, policies and products, and its answers age. The monthly fee mostly exists for that.

## What sits outside

The WhatsApp Business API cost, billed by Meta per conversation, is passed through at its real value. Integrations with closed systems that have no API are assessed separately, because they require a more fragile alternative route.

## What makes a project expensive

**Number of integrations.** An agent that only converses is simple. One that checks stock in an ERP, writes to a CRM and files something in tax software is three projects.

**Conversation volume.** This changes infrastructure and API cost, not development cost.

**Volume of exceptions.** The more edge cases the agent must resolve alone, the more configuration and testing. This is where budgets overrun.

## When not to hire

If one person handles today's volume with time to spare, automation solves little. If the process still changes weekly, stabilise the process first — automating a mess only makes the mess faster.
