## The difference in one conversation

A customer writes: *"bought yesterday and nothing has arrived, it should have been here by now right"*.

The **chatbot** looks for that phrase in its flow, finds nothing and replies with the menu: *"Press 1 for Sales, 2 for Support"*.

The **AI agent** understands this is about an order, identifies the customer, checks tracking, sees the package is in transit arriving tomorrow, and says exactly that — alongside the original delivery estimate, to address the implied complaint.

## When a chatbot still wins

Short, closed, very high volume processes. Confirming attendance, choosing between three options, looking up one value. A fixed flow is cheaper to maintain and never surprises anyone — and predictability has value.

## The common mistake

Hiring an AI agent for something that was a form. And the opposite, more expensive mistake: keeping a menu chatbot in an operation where customers write in plain language — every off-script message becomes a human ticket, and the automation saved nothing.
