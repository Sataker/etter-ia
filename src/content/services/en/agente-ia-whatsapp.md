## When it makes sense

If message volume has passed what your team can handle, if service stops at 6pm while customers buy at 11pm, or if most incoming questions are the same question — an agent solves that without hiring.

## What it is not

It is not a "press 1 for sales" phone menu. It is not a bot that answers anything to look intelligent. And it does not replace your team: it removes repetitive work from humans and hands over a mature conversation to whoever closes the deal.
