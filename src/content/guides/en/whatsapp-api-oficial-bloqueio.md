## Why unofficial tools get numbers banned

Unofficial tools work by keeping a session that pretends to be a mobile phone. From Meta's side the pattern is detectable: high volume, identical messages, regular sending times. When the system classifies that as unauthorised automation, the number is banned — without warning and usually taking the history with it.

The cost of that ban is rarely the number itself. It is the base of customer conversations that lived inside it.

## How the official route works

The WhatsApp Business API is the authorised path. The number goes through verification, the business is identified, and Meta bills per conversation. In exchange, automation is permitted — that is what it exists for.

There is one rule people tend to discover late: outside the 24-hour window after the customer's last message, you can only send pre-approved message templates. That limits mass blasting, which is exactly what keeps the channel's read rates high.

## Rule of thumb

If the proposal you were shown involves scanning a QR code to connect a phone, it is unofficial. It may work for months and stop on any given day.
