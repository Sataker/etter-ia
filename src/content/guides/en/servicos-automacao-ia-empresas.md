## The five categories

**AI customer service agents.** They handle WhatsApp, Instagram and website chat: answering questions, qualifying leads, booking and handing over to humans when needed. The most common entry point, because the bottleneck is visible and results show within weeks.

**Custom applications and platforms.** For when no ready-made system fits the operation, or when the internal process is the differentiator itself. Longer timeline, more structural return.

**Automated dashboards and reports.** The data already exists, scattered across five systems. Automation collects, consolidates and delivers it finished, with alerts when an indicator moves off track.

**Systems integration.** The lowest cost and highest return in most companies: making current tools talk so data gets entered once.

**Sales automation.** Qualification, instant replies, proposals and follow-up. The bigger gain usually comes from no longer losing leads who went unanswered, not from selling more to those who already reply.

## How to choose where to start

List the tasks that consume the most team hours. Mark the ones that follow clear rules and happen many times a week. Start with whatever sits at the top of both lists — that is where automation pays back fastest, and where a mistake costs least if the estimate turns out optimistic.
